YanYu Chen has broad interests in insect systematics, community ecology, insect biogeography and behavioural ecology. His undergraduate research project focuses on the global patterns of co-extinction risk in mutualistic networks, and the reshaping rules of predation networks along environmental gradients. He is currently learning quantitative modelling and statistical methods for ecological network analysis.

During coursework at Nanjing University (南京大学), he built up knowledge in community ecology and biostatistics, preparing for future field sampling and data analysis. YanYu is a nature enthusiast and enjoys observing insects in nature whenever possible.
