QinRu is interested in how microclimate shapes species persistence and interactions in urban areas under climate change. He wants to first understand what lies between the macroclimate and the conditions species actually experience—which may be unexpectedly mild or extreme. On top of that information, he will further investigate how insects navigate within and adapt to urban microclimate, ultimately helping us to design microclimate refugia for conserving biodiversity in urban heat islands. 

He obtained his Bachelor’s Degree at Shanghai Ocean University (上海海洋大学). He enjoys bird watching and sudoku.
