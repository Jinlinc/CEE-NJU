I am an environmental science researcher and educator, Associate Professor at the College of Eco-Environmental Engineering, Qinghai University since 2018. I completed his BSc, MSc and PhD in Environmental Science at the College of Earth and Environmental Sciences, Lanzhou University, under the supervision of Prof. Zhongren Nan. My long-standing research interest is material cycling in ecosystems and pollution prevention and control. I join the CEE Lab as a visiting scholar from September 2026, working on population dynamics under exogenous pollution stress and on species interactions. This direction builds on my previous research while bringing in new ecological thinking — and I find it genuinely exciting! I very much welcome guidance from senior colleagues and exchange with peers, making progress in science together.

I enjoy badminton, basketball and table tennis, and I am also a regular at theatre and film — the more "artsy" side of life. Above all, I love delicious food. I hope to grow professionally and, along the way, to make friends who share the same interests!
